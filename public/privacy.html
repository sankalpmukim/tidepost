Tidepost Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Tidepost ("the app") is an email client hosted at https://tidepost.sankalpmukim.dev and operated by Sankalp Mukim (sankalpmukim@gmail.com, "the operator"). This policy explains how the app accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes Google user data.

Google user data we access

When you connect a Google account, Tidepost requests the following OAuth scopes:

  • openid, email, profile — your Google account email address, display name, and profile picture URL, used to identify your account inside the app.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify — the contents of your Gmail mailbox: message subjects, senders and recipients, message bodies, attachments, labels, and read/unread and draft state. This scope is required for the app's core purpose of displaying, searching, organizing, composing, and sending email on your behalf.

How we use Google user data

  • Mail list, search, and reading. Message metadata and bodies are fetched from Gmail and indexed so the app can show your inbox, threads, and search results quickly.
  • Sending and organizing. The app composes, replies to, drafts, and sends email through Gmail, and syncs read/unread state, labels, and deletions back to your mailbox.
  • Assisted writing (optional). If you configure an Anthropic API key, thread text you explicitly select may be sent to Anthropic solely to generate a brief, draft, or plan you requested. This is off unless you enable it.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training any AI/ML model. The app requests only the scopes needed for the features above.

Sharing with third parties

Tidepost does not sell, rent, or share your Google user data with third parties. The only third party that may process your data is:

  • Anthropic — and only if you explicitly configure an Anthropic API key and request an AI-assisted brief, draft, or plan. Only the thread text needed for that request is sent.

Infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, which hosts the app) process data solely to operate the service. Google itself continues to process your mailbox under your Google account settings.

Storage and protection

  • Mail index and account data are stored in the app's server-side storage (Cloudflare D1 database and Durable Objects), accessible only to your logged-in session.
  • OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • All traffic to and from the app is served over HTTPS.
  • Each connected mailbox's data is isolated per user account; no data is shared across user accounts.

Retention and deletion

  • Your mail index is retained only while your account is connected, so the app can keep showing your mailbox. It is not kept after you disconnect.
  • You can delete your data at any time: disconnect an account (or delete your Tidepost account) from the app's settings. This removes the stored mail index and stored OAuth tokens from the app's storage. It does not delete anything from your Gmail mailbox itself.
  • To request deletion or a copy of your data, email sankalpmukim@gmail.com and it will be handled promptly.
