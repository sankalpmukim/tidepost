Tidepost

Tidepost is a keyboard-first email client for Gmail and Outlook.

Tidepost sorts what needs your answer from what can wait, keeps 100-message threads readable, and checks every send before it leaves. You sign in with your own Google or Microsoft account, and Tidepost reads, organizes, and sends your mail on your behalf.

Open Tidepost

What Tidepost does

  • Triage lanes — every inbox thread lands in Needs you, Waiting, FYI, or Updates, decided from your mail headers.
  • Readable long threads — older messages collapse, quoted history folds away, and a thread chart shows who said what.
  • Briefs and plans — a short summary of where a thread stands, with every claim cited to its message.
  • Safe sending — outside-domain recipients, large recipient lists, and missing attachments are checked before anything leaves.
  • Works with your provider — Gmail and Microsoft 365 accounts, search on the provider, and a local index that stays fast.

Who it is for

People who get a lot of corporate mail and want to clear it with the keyboard instead of the mouse.
